A visualization of the [Hopf fibration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hopf_fibration): $S^1\hookrightarrow S^3\xrightarrow {\ p\,} S^2$, where we regard $S^3$ as the one-point compactification of $\mathbb{R}^3$.
