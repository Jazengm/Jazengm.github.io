## Description

This seminar has concluded. It focused on the basic definitions of mixed Hodge structures and their construction on general varieties, with an emphasis on concrete examples. The schedule and materials below are retained as an archive.

## Spacetime

Former meeting time: Fridays, 3:30–5:00 p.m., Evans 762. No further meetings are scheduled.
